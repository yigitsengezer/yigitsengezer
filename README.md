## 🧑‍💻 About

I work as a security consultant and hunt on YesWeHack and Intigriti in my own time.
Most of what I publish here started as something I needed during an engagement. I'm currently
looking into where AI agents are actually useful in vulnerability research, and where they
just make confident noise.

Notes and write-ups go on the [blog](https://yigitsengezer.gitbook.io/).
